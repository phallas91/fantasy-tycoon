Overview and local game data

Arcane Trade Empire is a single-player idle tycoon game. Core gameplay requires no game account. Progress, settings, achievements, entitlements and anti-tamper metadata are stored locally. The developer does not automatically receive local save files. Players can erase local progress in the game settings.

Optional cloud save

If Google Play Games cloud save is enabled and the player signs in, an encoded game snapshot and progress metadata are processed through Google Play Games to back up and restore progress. This feature remains inactive in builds where Play Games Services is not configured.

Advertising and consent

If Google Mobile Ads is enabled, rewarded ads are optional and requested by the player. Google may process IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents consent choices and the game provides access to privacy options.

In-app purchases

Google Play processes purchases. The game receives product identifiers, purchase states and tokens required to grant, acknowledge, restore and prevent duplicate delivery. The developer does not receive full payment-card details.

Notifications and permissions

The app may schedule local reminders after permission is granted. Core gameplay does not request precise GPS location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or health data.

Retention, deletion and rights

Local data remains until progress is reset, app storage is cleared or the app is removed, subject to operating-system backups. Google service data follows Google's retention controls and legal requirements. Privacy and deletion requests can be sent to [REQUIRED: PRIVACY CONTACT].
